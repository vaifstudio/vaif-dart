# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the
project's issues, pull requests and other spaces, and to anyone representing
the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, accept that people hold different
views and have different levels of experience, and do not harass, insult or
publish the private information of others.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to support@vaif.studio. Reports are reviewed and
handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code and issues that do not follow this code of conduct, and may
restrict or ban participants who violate it, following the enforcement
guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
